Get help

For questions, bug reports, or feedback, email mfpbuilds@gmail.com.

Setting up your trackers

Follow the setup guide to copy the spreadsheet template and connect its tabs. Keep the header row and the order of columns A through N in both trackers.

Google Sheets connection

Make sure the Google account you connect can edit your spreadsheet. Paste the spreadsheet URL in Settings, select the correct tab for each tracker, and save your settings.

Autofill is in beta

Autofill support varies by website. Review every filled field before submitting an application. You can continue using job saving and outreach tracking without adding an autofill profile.

Reporting a problem

Include the extension version, your Chrome version, the feature you were using, what you expected, and what happened. If useful, attach a screenshot with personal details removed.

Please do not send passwords, authorization tokens, private spreadsheet contents, or sensitive profile information.

Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy for details about local profile storage, Google Sheets access, and usage feedback. Contact the same email address for privacy questions or requests concerning an email address you supplied with feedback.
