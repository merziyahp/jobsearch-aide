Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

What this extension does

JobSearch Aide is a Chrome extension that helps you organize your job search. It saves job listing details to a Google Sheet you configure, downloads a copy of the job description to your local downloads folder, and lets you log outreach contacts to a second sheet.

What data we access

  • Google Sheets: We access only the specific Google Sheet(s) you connect during setup. We read and write job listing and outreach data to those sheets. We do not access any other files in your Google account.
  • Active browser tab: When you click the extension, we read the content of the current page to extract job listing information. We do not store or transmit your browsing history.
  • Downloads: We save job description files to your local downloads folder. These files never leave your device.
  • Local storage: We store your extension settings (Sheet URL, tab name, autofill profile) locally in your browser. This data never leaves your device.

Anonymous usage counts and feedback

To learn whether JobSearch Aide is actually useful, the extension keeps simple counts in your browser: how many times, and on how many separate days, you've used Save & Log, outreach logging, and autofill, and whether you've opened or completed setup.

After you've used a feature on a few different days, the extension may ask once (and at most twice in total) whether it's useful, with a thumbs up or down and an optional comment. When you answer, skip, or close that question, these items are sent to a Google Sheet owned by the developer:

  • A random ID generated when you install the extension. It isn't linked to your name, Google account, or browser.
  • The usage counts described above, how many days since install, and the extension version.
  • Your answer: thumbs up or down, and any optional reason or comment you typed.
  • Your email address, only if you tick "OK to email me about this" and enter it.

This never includes company names, job titles, job descriptions, page addresses, LinkedIn contacts, your Google Sheet details, or anything from your autofill profile. Nothing is sent until a feedback question is shown.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your job search data, browsing history, or autofill profile.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • Your job and outreach data goes only to your own Google Sheet or your local machine.

Equal opportunity information

If you use the autofill feature and enter equal opportunity information (gender, race, disability status, veteran status), that data is stored only in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). It is never synced across devices and is never transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

This extension uses the Google Sheets API to read and write data to your Google Sheet. Your use of Google Sheets is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Anonymous feedback is received by a Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet controlled by the developer. If you shared your email with feedback and want it removed, email the address below.
